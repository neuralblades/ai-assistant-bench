The Eiffel Tower is located in Paris, France. It was built by Gustave Eiffel
and completed in 1889 for the World's Fair. It stands 330 meters tall and
was the tallest man-made structure in the world for 41 years.

The capital of Australia is Canberra, not Sydney. Canberra was purpose-built
as the capital and became the seat of government in 1927.

Python was created by Guido van Rossum and first released in 1991.
It emphasizes code readability and simplicity.

The Berlin Wall fell on November 9, 1989, marking the end of the Cold War era.
It had divided East and West Berlin since 1961.

Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius (212 Fahrenheit) at sea level.
At higher altitudes, it boils at lower temperatures due to reduced air pressure.
